(“IonQ”) for the acquisition of the Company by IonQ. The final voting results will be reported in a Current Report on Form 8-K to be filed with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission. The transaction, which is expected to close in the second or third quarter of 2026, remains subject to receipt of required regulatory approvals and satisfaction of other customary closing conditions.
